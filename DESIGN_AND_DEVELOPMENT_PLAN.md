## 1. Design Review & Creative Analysis (Sample Reference: Refit)

The reference design stands out through distinct geometric treatments, strong contrast transitions, and purposeful typography. For Dominion Integrated Electrical & Engineering Limited, we adapt and elevate these core visual motifs using Dominion's brand identity:

### Key Design Motifs & Engineering Translations
1. **Geometric Chamfered Badges & Section Markers:**
   * *Reference:* Teal polygon/chamfered pill tags (`ABOUT US`, `SERVICES`, `PROCESS`).
   * *Dominion Translation:* Precision-cut architectural badges using Dominion Royal Blue (`#0F2B82`) with Gold/Amber (`#D99B26`) edge accents and subtle industrial grid coordinates (e.g., `// 01. CIVIL & STRUCTURAL`).
2. **Architectural Chamfered & Clip-Path Image Containers:**
   * *Reference:* Images with bottom-left and bottom-right 45-degree diagonal cut corners.
   * *Dominion Translation:* CSS `clip-path` masks with diagonal chamfers that simulate technical blueprints, structural steel cuts, and industrial panels.
3. **High-Contrast Section Rhythms:**
   * Alternate between deep midnight navy/dark slate (`#09122C` / `#0A192F`) and ultra-clean architectural white/off-white (`#F8FAFC`).
4. **Numbered Process Cards with Blueprint Wireframe Typography:**
   * Large outline/wireframe stroke numerals (`01`, `02`, `03`, `04`, `05`) representing engineering stages: Consult $\rightarrow$ Design $\rightarrow$ Cost & Compliance $\rightarrow$ Execute $\rightarrow$ Commission.
5. **Floating Hybrid Cards & Overlapping Content Blocks:**
   * Elevated cards that bridge dark and light sections with 3D depth, border glows, and industrial accent lines.
6. **Diamond Emblem / Structural Rhombus Masks:**
   * Use rotated rhombus frames and badge cutouts for certifications, client logos, and production facility stamps.
7. **Minimalist Technical Forms:**
   * Clean architectural underline inputs with animated focus indicators for RFQ (Request for Quote) submissions.

## 4. Technical Architecture, SEO & Performance Strategy

1. **Framework & Engine:**
   * **Next.js (App Router)** with TypeScript.
   * **Tailwind CSS** with custom geometric utility plugins (`clip-path` polygon utilities).
   * **Lucide React** for crisp engineering iconography.
2. **Performance & Image Optimizations:**
   * Conversion of image assets to Next.js responsive optimized formats (WebP/AVIF).
   * Fast initial server-side rendering (SSR) and static generation (SSG) for 95+ Google Lighthouse score.
3. **SEO & Structured Data:**
   * Schema.org `Organization`, `LocalBusiness`, and `EngineeringCompany` JSON-LD structured data.
   * OpenGraph & Twitter cards for social sharing.
   * Geo-targeting tags for Oyo State, Ibadan, Lagos, and Nigeria-wide infrastructure keywords.
4. **Admin Panel / CMS Capabilities:**
   * Content schema to allow easy updates to Projects, Precast Products, Team Profiles, and Contact details without touching code.
